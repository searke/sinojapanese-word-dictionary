Kanji neighbourhoods

Think of every kanji as a town and every two-kanji word as a road between two towns. Nearly all kanji end up in one big web, and the web falls naturally into neighbourhoods: groups of kanji that form far more words with each other than with the rest. The groups were found automatically (Louvain community detection); the names are hand-picked to fit. See them laid out on the map.
