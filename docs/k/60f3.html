How are levels decided?

Levels measure how easy a word is to read, not how common it is. Each kanji gets a level from its Japanese school grade, its old JLPT level and how often it appears in newspapers:

  • Beginner — the ~250 most basic kanji (old JLPT 4–3)
  • Elementary — taught in Japanese elementary school
  • Intermediate — secondary-school jōyō kanji in common use
  • Advanced — the remaining jōyō kanji
  • Rare — uses a kanji outside the jōyō list

A word's level is the level of its harder kanji. So 食事 is Beginner (both kanji are basic), while 飽食 is Advanced because 飽 is a less common kanji.
